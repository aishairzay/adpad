# Deal emails

A sponsor or creator turns on "Email me when my deals move" on My campaigns or My listings. That writes dealMailPrefs/<their wallet> with their verified sign-in email; the rules refuse any other address, and wallet-only accounts cannot turn it on. Every 5 minutes the dealMailer function (run as me) compares each sponsorDeals status with dealMailLog/<dealId> and emails each opted-in party once per change, from the app's mail address, with the headline, amount, deadline or payout time and a link to My campaigns. The brief and proof text never go into an email. Which party hears about which status is in functions/dealMailText.ts. A deal first seen more than 30 minutes after its last change is noted without mailing, so old deals are not mailed. Send results show in the app's mailbox.
